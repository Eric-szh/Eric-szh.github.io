Projects

ICU Patient Allocation via Restless Multi-Armed Bandits

eICU · 2025 · github.com/Eric-szh/RMAB-MIMIC

Formulated ICU bed allocation as a finite-horizon RMAB over an 82-state MDP, estimated transition matrices from 17,885 eICU patient-day transitions, and derived a Q-function heuristic policy with value iteration. Over a 90-day horizon (N=300, 20 seeds) the policy reduced simulated deaths by about 39 versus a sickest-first baseline. An ethnicity-augmented variant showed how a policy learned from observational data reproduces the disparities already in it.

RL · MDP · value iteration · fairness · healthcare

RGB–LiDAR Fusion for Food Macro-Nutrient Estimation

Nutrition5k · 2026

Dual-stream PyTorch model: a pretrained ResNet18 for RGB and a purpose-built 4-layer CNN for depth, fused into a regression head. Cut calorie MAE 17.6% (57.70 to 47.52 kcal) over an RGB-only baseline. Ablations showed the lightweight depth encoder beats a full ResNet18 depth branch, and that Food-101 pre-training hurts portion regression.
